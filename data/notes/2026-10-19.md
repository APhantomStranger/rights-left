# It’s Surreal That We Have to Worry About This

To set the table for this one... I never thought I’d have to type this sentence as an American: we may have to seriously contend with the idea that an election in this country gets tampered with. Not “contested.” Not “close.” Tampered with. I grew up in a house of teachers, where civics wasn’t some dusty chapter you skimmed before the test... it was the deal. You vote, they count it, the loser concedes, and we all go to work on Wednesday. We were the country that sent observers to OTHER countries to make sure their elections were clean [check]. And now I’m reading headlines about Trump reviving his 2020 conspiracy theories with the midterms looming, and my first reaction isn’t even anger. It’s this stunned, numb disbelief... like standing on Alderaan, watching the Death Star swing around, and realizing nobody in the room seems all that bothered.

So this one goes out to the MAGA crowd. If you’re this comfortable with election interference, Moscow is right there. Pack a bag. I’m sure Putin would love to have you (he’s had decades of practice running elections where the winner is never in doubt). Because let’s call it what it is: Trump supporters have betrayed this country and the ideals it’s supposed to stand for. And here’s the question I can’t get a straight answer to... if Trump can dismantle the Constitution and voting rights, and you cheer him on while he does it, then WHAT is it about America you love? The flag? The bumper sticker? Because the thing that flag stands for IS the Constitution. It IS the vote. Strip those out and what’s left isn’t a country... it’s a mascot.

— Chris
