# Now Show Your Work

I'm still riding high from last week. Voters handed Democrats control of both the House and the Senate, and Republicans failed to flip a single seat and what a sense of catharsis! After nearly two years of watching this administration treat the law, the courts and Congress itself as obstacles, the country said enough. It was a clear verdict, and it felt good. But winning is the easy part. When the new Congress is sworn in on January 3, the real test begins, and I really need Democrats to meet it in a way we haven't seen before.

That starts with transparency. A problem I hear and feel is that normal everyday people don’t know or see what’s being worked on. With Trump in office there were constant, large news events of him changing and removing cornerstones of our democracy.  I want this new majority talking to the American people every single day: what they're investigating, what they've subpoenaed, what bills are moving and why, all in plain language, through town halls, livestreams and straight talk instead of press-release fog. Congress was built to be the strongest branch of government. It holds the power of the purse, the power to investigate and the power to check a president who oversteps. This week the White House already warned Democrats they could face criminal inquiries just for doing oversight. I am reminded of that scene in Lord of the Rings: Return of the King, where Sauron is surrounded in his tower.  The answer is to do that work out in the open, where the public can see it and stand behind it, and to remind the whole nation what a coequal branch of government looks like.

— Chris
